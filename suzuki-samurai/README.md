# Suzuki Samurai

Parts for a 1986 Suzuki Samurai.

The Samurai is old enough that plenty of small pieces are simply not sold anymore, and where they are, you often have to buy a whole assembly to get the one piece you needed. These are the ones I have had to make.

Everything here is measured off my own vehicle. Samurai, SJ413 and Sierra vary by market and year, so measure before you print if yours is not the same.

## Parts

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Mirror spacer](mirror-spacer) | Spacer under the interior rearview mirror mounting screw |
| [Instrument cluster bolt cap](instrument-cluster-bolt-cap) | Cap over a bolt on the instrument cluster board, so it cannot short against anything behind the dash |
